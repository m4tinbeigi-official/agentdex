# Turnitout AI Text Humanizer

Use when drafting content that must avoid generic AI cadence, repetitive transition markers, and synthetic predictability flagged by Turnitin, GPTZero, or CopyLeaks.

## Stylometric Principles
1. **Burstiness Variance**: Alternate between short punchy sentences (3 to 6 words) and extended complex thoughts (20+ words).
2. **Perplexity Elevation**: Replace generic corporate transitions ("Moreover", "Furthermore", "In conclusion", "It is important to remember") with organic conversational or direct transitions.
3. **Fluff Stripping**: Eliminate passive boilerplate and abstract summaries.
4. **Idiosyncratic Phrasing**: Retain domain-specific, direct tone over generic diplomatic neutrality.

## Text Transformation Rules
- Detect and replace statistical word triplets favored by LLMs.
- Inject deliberate cadence breaks and rhetorical pauses.
- Remove symmetry in paragraph lengths: keep some single-line paragraphs and others descriptive.
- Preserve technical definitions verbatim; alter only syntactic scaffolding.
